# Renewal Desk: release notes

*For Lerato, GM Virtual Sales · October 2026*

The Renewal Desk is ready for real customer data.

**What your team gets**
- One list of every customer whose contract ends in the next 90 days, in order, with the customers who can renew today at the top.
- One page per customer: their lines, who to speak to and whether we may message them, and every call so far.
- A call can no longer be logged as "call back" without a date, so follow-ups stop slipping.
- Messages can only go to customers who have agreed, and never to anyone on Legal's opt-out list. The Desk queues them; the Network's platform sends them.

**What you get as manager**
- An exceptions page: overdue callbacks, customers who can renew but have no next step, customers we cannot reach, and anyone not yet allocated to an agent.
- Upload the Network's monthly file yourself. Uploading the same file twice is harmless, call history is never lost, and if the Network changes the file's layout the Desk stops and tells you which column changed instead of loading bad data.

**What we fixed before go-live**
The first version could have exposed customer details to anyone on the internet and let any agent see everyone's calls. Both are closed, and the access key that was accidentally published has been replaced.

**Not in this release**
Automatic WhatsApp or SMS campaigns. We recommend switching them on once at least 60% of customers in the renewal window have a confirmed contact.

**Decision needed from you:** which agent should own the 15 new customers in each month's file by default (today the manager allocates them on the exceptions page).
